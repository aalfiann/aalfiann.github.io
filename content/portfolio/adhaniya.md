Adhaniya is an Islamic prayer times PWA with adhan alarm, qibla direction, islamic calendar and multi-language support.
<!--more-->

Features:  
- Adhan with Alarm
- Push Notification FCM
- Multi Calculation Method
- Auto Geolocation
- Manual set location
- Qibla Direction
- Islamic Calendar
- Multi Language Support
- Offline Support
- etc
