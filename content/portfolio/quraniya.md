Quraniya is an offline-first Quran reader with multiple translations, tafsir, colored tajweed and murrotal audio.
<!--more-->

**Mission**  
Quraniya aims to make the Quran accessible, understandable, and available to everyone — anytime, anywhere, even without an internet connection.

**Vision**  
To build a free, trusted, and privacy-friendly Quran companion that helps people stay connected with the Quran throughout their daily lives.

Quraniya is built with the hope that its benefits can continue to reach people long after it is created. We believe that providing useful access to the Quran is more important than building a commercial product.

**Features:**  
- Complete 114 surahs (available in 20 translations)
- Include with Tafsir multi sources (available in 15 translations)
- 9 Color-coded Tajweed (ghunnah, qalqalah, madd, idgham bi-ghunnah, idgham bila-ghunnah, iqlab, ikhfa haqiqi, ikhfa syafawi and idgham mitslain)
- Focus mode for reading without translations
- Switch translations directly on the reading page
- Verse-by-verse murattal audio (online streaming but auto downloaded into storage)
- Surah search & verse navigation
- Bookmarks & reading progress
- Share verses with their translations
- Offline-first with automatic updates
- Light, dark & system themes
